Generated by `npm run registry:build`. Inputs: `docs/historical_data/*.json` (read-only), `overrides.json`, `names_fa.json`.

## Numbers
- 1,045 curated entries → 459 raw dynasty names → **238 dynasties**, **1,050 realm rows** (some entries list disjoint spans).
- 1 entry deliberately skipped: *Golden Horde* in `1375–1468-AD.json` has years `"Intermittent"` (no dates). Listed in `overrides.json → skip`.
- 34 region ids outside the 12 board regions (satrapies/provinces) are kept with `core: false`.
- Families: persian 118, turkic 45, foreign 35, nomadic 19, arab 10, greek 6, semitic 5.

### Hand check — 1000 AD (17)
Registry: Bagratid Armenia, Bavandids, Buyids, Ghaznavids, Hasanwayhids, Justanids, Ma'danids, Ma'munids, Paduspanids,
Qarakhanids, Rawwadids, Saffarids, Sallarids, Shaddadids, Shirvanshahs, Sogdian city-states, Ziyarids.
Historical expectation for the plateau around 1000: the Buyids (Rayy/Fars/Iraq), Ghaznavids (Khorasan, east),
Qarakhanids (Transoxiana, Samanids fell 999), Ziyarids (Gorgan), Bavandids (Tabaristan), Justanids (Daylam),
Rawwadids and Sallarids (Azerbaijan), Shaddadids and Shirvanshahs (Arran/Shirvan), Hasanwayhids (Kurdistan),
Ma'munids (Khwarazm, from 995), Saffarids (Sistan). All present. Caveats below.

## Known gaps / things a historian should look at
1. **Abbasid Caliphate** is absent at 1000 in Mesopotamia: the curated data has no Abbasid entry covering that region/year.
2. **Sogdian City-States** spans −600 → 1099 because merged variants include "11th century"; too broad to read as one polity.
3. **1750 = 41** is real fragmentation (Zand, Afsharid remnants, ~25 khanates) but also inflated by one-off tribal/khanate entries.
4. **500 BC = 10** is low versus the raw 17 because Achaemenid vassal/satrapy variants are now one dynasty — intended, but the
   satrapy detail is kept as `relation: vassal` / `core: false` rows.
5. **Persian names (`names_fa.json`) were drafted by an AI assistant and are NOT reviewed** by a native speaker/historian.
6. **Family (colour) assignments** are partly interpretive (e.g. Kushans = foreign, Georgian kingdoms = foreign, Buyids = persian).
   All overrides are in `overrides.json → family`.
7. Overlord links (`overrides.json → overlord`) and capitals are **not populated yet** (the curated data has no capitals;
   capitals come from the board work in Slice 3/4).
8. Wikidata ruler dates (`npm run registry:wikidata`) have **not been fetched**: `www.wikidata.org` is blocked in the cloud
   sandbox and `data/registry/ruler_qids.json` still needs curated QIDs.

## Parser fix that came with this slice
The old year parser in `historicalDataIndex.ts` read 23 mixed BC/AD ranges backwards (e.g. `c. 140 BC – 224 AD` became
224 BC → 140 BC) and dropped `present`/century forms. `src/lib/yearRange.ts` replaces it and is shared by the AI-anchor index
and the registry. This also changes which dynasties the AI prompt sees for the Parthian/Kushan era — a correction.
